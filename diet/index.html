SLEEP DIET GUIDE

What you eat can shape how well you sleep.

Build a sleep-friendly plate with nourishing choices, thoughtful timing, and fewer late-night stimulants. Small, consistent changes matter more than any single “sleep food.”

Explore the guide

Gentle evening choices

Foods that may support sleep

GOOD · SUPPORTIVE OPTIONS
🥝Good

Kiwi

A light fruit with fiber and antioxidants. Some small studies suggest kiwi before bed may support sleep, though results can vary.

🍒Good

Tart cherry juice

Tart cherries naturally contain melatonin. Choose a small serving and watch added sugar and fluid timing.

🥜Good

Almonds

A small handful offers magnesium, healthy fats, and protein for a satisfying snack without a heavy meal.

🌰Good

Walnuts

Walnuts provide healthy fats and small amounts of melatonin; enjoy them as part of a balanced snack.

🍌Good

Bananas

A convenient source of carbohydrates and potassium that works well as a light evening bite if you are hungry.

🥣Good

Oatmeal

A modest serving of warm oats is filling and easy to pair with fruit or nuts for a simple snack.

Mind the timing

Foods and drinks to limit near bedtime

AVOID · ESPECIALLY LATE
☕Avoid late

Coffee

Caffeine can stay active for hours. Consider a cutoff at least 8 hours before your intended bedtime.

⚡Avoid late

Energy drinks

Often high in caffeine and sugar, they may make it harder to wind down and stay asleep.

🍷Avoid

Alcohol

It may make you feel sleepy at first, but alcohol can fragment sleep later in the night.

🍰Limit late

Sugary desserts

Large, very sweet treats close to bedtime can feel uncomfortable and disrupt your wind-down routine.

🌶️Limit late

Spicy foods

Spicy or heavy meals may trigger indigestion or raise body temperature for some people.
