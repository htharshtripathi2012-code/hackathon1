SLEEP TIPS

Small routines that make your mornings feel easier.

Better sleep usually comes from a few consistent habits, not a perfect schedule. Try these practical, low-stress habits to help your body settle earlier and wake up more refreshed.

Quick win

Aim for a 30-minute wind-down.

Dim lights, put your phone away, and let your brain switch from work mode to rest mode before bed.

Keep a steady rhythm

Go to bed and wake up around the same time, even on weekends, so your sleep pressure stays predictable.

Cut screen glow

Blue light can push your body clock later. Try dimming screens or enabling night mode in the last hour before sleep.

Make the room sleep-friendly

Cooler temperatures, blackout curtains, and fewer distractions can help your body feel safe enough to drift off.

Keep evenings calm

A warm shower, light stretching, or a short wind-down ritual can prime your nervous system for rest.

The three best habits

  • 1

    Set a consistent sleep window

    A regular bedtime helps your body clock build a reliable sleep rhythm over time.

  • 2

    Protect the hour before sleep

    Avoid stimulating content, heavy meals, and intense workouts close to bedtime when possible.

  • 3

    Build a repeatable evening ritual

    The same cues — tea, reading, gentle stretches, dim lights — can tell your brain it is time to rest.

What to avoid

Late caffeine or energy drinks in the afternoon or evening.
Stress-heavy scrolling right before bed when your mind is still racing.
Very bright lights or noisy environments that keep your body alert.
Sleeping at inconsistent times if you want more stable deep sleep and REM cycles.
